Settings & backup

Data backup

Your records stay in this browser on this device. To move to another device, export a JSON backup here and restore that file on the other device. Restore replaces the current records, after confirmation.

Owner's investment

Set the personal money contributed before using this tracker. This is separate from sales and ordinary expenses. Additional personal contributions should be recorded as Money in → Additional capital.

Current baseline: ₹3,50,380. Today's ₹34,030 stock purchase funded by sales remains an inventory purchase and does not increase owner's investment.

Included categories

Money in: Sales, Other income, Refund received, Additional capital. Only Sales counts as revenue.
Money out: Inventory purchase, Rent, Electricity, Store expenses, Transport, Packaging, Marketing, Other expense.
